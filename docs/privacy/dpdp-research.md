# DPDP research and implementation baseline

**Reviewed:** 24 August 2026  
**Scope:** vamsimarripudi.tech public website and contact channel  
**Status:** operational guidance, not legal advice or a statement of full legal compliance.

## Official source position

- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 allows the Central Government to appoint different commencement dates for different provisions: [official Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf).
- Gazette notification G.S.R. 843(E), dated 13 November 2025, commenced selected Act provisions on publication, scheduled section 6(9) and section 27(1)(d) for one year after publication, and scheduled the main operational provisions—including sections 3–5, most of section 6, and sections 7–17—for eighteen months after publication: [official enforcement timeline](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf).
- Gazette notification G.S.R. 846(E), dated 13 November 2025, gives the same phased approach for the DPDP Rules, 2025: Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 on publication; Rule 4 one year later; and Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 eighteen months later: [official Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

## Practical dates used here

| Date | Position relevant to this site |
| --- | --- |
| 13 November 2025 | The listed initial Act and Rules provisions commenced. |
| 13 November 2026 | The one-year provisions in G.S.R. 843(E) and Rule 4 of the Rules are scheduled to commence. |
| 13 May 2027 | The eighteen-month provisions in G.S.R. 843(E) and Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 are scheduled to commence. |

## Implementation stance

The public Privacy Notice and contact-form notice are implemented in advance because clear, purpose-specific communication, minimal collection, secure delivery, retention controls, and a contact path are sound operational practice. The public website must not claim “DPDP compliant,” “fully compliant,” or that a statutory request period applies before it can be verified as effective and applicable.

## Ongoing review trigger

Review this material before 13 November 2026, before 13 May 2027, after any change to form fields/processors/analytics, and after any security incident. Obtain qualified Indian privacy/legal advice before relying on this as a formal compliance assessment.
