# Retention policy

## Rules used by the site

1. **Contact email correspondence:** Review for deletion or archiving 24 months after the last meaningful interaction. Keep longer only where required for a genuine legal obligation, active security investigation, or dispute.
2. **Rate-limit entries:** Delete automatically through in-memory expiry after at most 15 minutes.
3. **Duplicate-submission fingerprints:** Delete automatically through in-memory expiry after at most 10 minutes.
4. **Local browser values:** Theme and optional game score remain on the visitor’s device until they clear browser data. The site does not receive these values.
5. **Provider logs:** Vercel and Resend retention are controlled by the applicable account configuration and provider service. The owner must review those settings at least annually and after a provider change.

## Operational owner task

At least quarterly, review old resolved contact threads in the enquiry mailbox and delete/archival-process those outside the stated retention period. This repository cannot automatically delete mail held by third-party mailbox providers.
