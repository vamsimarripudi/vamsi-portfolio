# Portfolio 4.0 + Personal Legal System — Product Requirements

Version: development draft 0.1 (2026-11-05). Source: Q1–Q63 approval register. This is an engineering specification, not legal advice or proof of compliance.

## Outcomes

1. Personal portfolio immediately answers who Vamsi is, what products are being built, and where visitors should go next.
2. A responsive, accessible engineering-canvas hero and evidence-first Work/Now pages retain existing colors and moderate typography.
3. Public /legal enables discovery of **actually existing** website notices; a new product-specific policy is added only after version-verified legal publication approval.
4. Independently secured legal backend and owner command workflows implement product-scoped requests, consent evidence, legal document revisions and governance.
5. Existing Corner, contact, survey, Enquiry Tracker and customer contractual rights remain unaffected unless a separately authorized integration is verified.

## Visitor personas and jobs to be done

- Recruiter: review experience, résumé, verified technical evidence and contact in few steps.
- Customer/prospect: learn PakkaFollow's *pre-launch* scope and find official product link without invented claims.
- Developer/collaborator: find architectural reasoning and publicly verifiable implementation decisions.
- Legal-policy reader: reach the correct existing or approved document; understand version, operator and contact path.
- Privacy complainant: submit an accessible request without a mandatory new product account; track only with verified authorization once backend is launched.
- Owner: review facts, documents, evidence and signoffs without allowing a content-edit permission to become legal-publication authority.

## Public surfaces

- Home: concise intro, Work/Résumé/Contact, PakkaFollow status, visitor-intent navigation and responsive lightweight SVG background.
- Work and Now: verified product facts, lifecycle distinctions, planning-stage teaser and engineering archive.
- /legal: fast searchable directory of **existing** public website notices; planned/unapproved policy records are not presented as effective.
- Existing /privacy and /terms: remain unchanged until exact-content preservation, legal review, and owner acceptance.
- Legal requests, policy versions and translation history: activate only after actual approved sources and secure backend are present.

## Private surfaces and boundaries

- Portfolio content lane: verified content facts; Git/versioned review and author-approved release.
- Legal lane: separate high-assurance identity, product authorization, append-only evidence, review and exact-hash approval.
- Customer data: product-scoped storage/lookup; no unrelated access, shared profiles, company products or automatic identity linking.
- Risk-sensitive controls: passkey/MFA, secure event ledger, encrypted evidence, backups, abuse prevention and service recovery are release gates, not cosmetic UI.

## Non-goals of the first development slice

- Publishing new policies, revising existing effective agreements, collecting identity documents, provisioning a live legal database, charging customers, sending outbound notices, or creating a legal officer designation.
- Claiming legal sufficiency, statutory compliance, live monitoring or 100% completion based on a mockup.
- Modifying Corner source or the existing open Corner PR.

## Definition of complete

Four independent status gates: Engineering Complete, Release Ready, Production Verified and Legal Publication Approved. Each requires its own proof. No critical/high blockers may remain for an affected release.
