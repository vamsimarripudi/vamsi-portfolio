# Public implementation documentation boundary

This repository is publicly readable. These design documents deliberately contain only the owner's approved high-level scope and safe architectural principles. They are **not** a production legal registry or a security-evidence vault.

Nonpublic product names, unreleased plans, customer cases, legal identity documents, statutory appointment evidence, keys, access rules, internal diagrams, security assessments, vendor contracts and any personally identifying records belong in a separately secured private system. They must never be copied here merely for convenience.

The two existing public portfolio notices remain at /privacy and /terms. A new legal policy cannot be labelled approved or published without verifiable independent legal review and explicit fresh owner approval. Branch pushes, draft pull requests and green checks confer no legal publication authority.
