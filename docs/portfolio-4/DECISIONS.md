## Enforcement rules

- APPROVED means a requirement is accepted; it does not mean operationally tested, legally reviewed, paid for, deployed or published.
- Every in-scope requirement must map to code, a test, proof, and exact commit, or to an identified external/owner/legal blocker.
- Product policy publication requires verified source evidence, qualified counsel review, a precise document hash and fresh owner authorization. No retroactive consent.
- Portfolio deployments, private legal API activation, product integrations, financial expenses and legal publication are distinct decisions.
- A developer may prepare code and run isolated tests but cannot silently change production, customer data, published contracts, or payment authorization.
- Company-operated products, services, secrets and data are out of scope.
