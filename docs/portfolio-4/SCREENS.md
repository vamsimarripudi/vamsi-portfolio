## Integrity rules

- Company-owned product identities and private customer/legal cases never enter this public source inventory.
- Only already public product names appear in the legal-directory frontend; unannounced projects remain in separately secured owner records.
- New legal documents are not generated, labeled current, or published by frontend design work.
- Dark mode and the original four brand colors are retained. Static/reduced-motion fallback remains mandatory.
