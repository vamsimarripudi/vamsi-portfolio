# PRD — Portfolio 4.0 and Central Personal Legal System

## Core outcome
A fast, clear founder-engineer portfolio with useful verified work; a **separately governed**, legally reviewed product-specific legal system. Public reading requires no account; sensitive legal requests demand proportionate verification; owner legal administration must be separately authenticated. No company-operated products.

## Personas and real journeys
- Recruiter → hero → verified experience / résumé → contact; no invented credentials or metrics.
- Buyer/client → PakkaFollow pre-launch case study → actual official product site → enquiry; never imply paid launch.
- Developer → engineering decisions, public code proof and technical writing; no private repo leakage.
- Product customer → direct product-specific approved policy → verified version and effective date → optional privacy/grievance action.
- Guest/former customer → account-free complaint intake → unique reference after durable storage → email verification → authorized status; no reference-only access.
- Individual operator → read-only dashboard → verified draft/evidence/counsel → fresh passkey/MFA → exact-hash owner approval and independently controlled publication.
- Counsel (when actually engaged) → recorded scope-qualified opinion on exact version; never automatically becomes grievance officer.

## Public pages and screen outcomes
Portfolio home: moderate type, existing colors, animated SVG/CSS engineering canvas that never covers CTAs. Work, Resume, Contact immediately visible. Visitor intent shortcuts without profiling. PakkaFollow correctly pre-launch, unnamed planning teaser. Work and project details show problem/solution/architecture/decisions, with verified media only. Now shows actual last-verified date and content. Writing/About/Engineering retain proper accessible navigation.
New /legal: only currently published or independently approved legal entries; search and product navigation for those entries, no unannounced products or unapproved drafts. Existing /privacy and /terms unchanged during initial migration. Future published legal artifacts require reviewed locale, historical versions, exact hashes and effective dates. Public hub links to actually verified fallback contact; it does not pretend the new request service exists until tested.
Future private: Owner portfolio content lane and independent owner legal lane, case queue, policy version diffs, legal source registry, vendor/privacy inventory, approvals, alerts and evidence. Private documents never in public bundle, search index or CDN.

## Key functional acceptance
1. Product registry private six, public list only approved discoverable products.
2. Exact document versions; no retroactive consent; separate optional data and payment authorizations.
3. Legal intake durable-before-reference, safe guest/session verification and product-scoped status.
4. Private encrypted evidence upload gated by verified scanner/storage, risk-justified.
5. Counsel-reviewed policy changes; hash-bound 10-minute owner authorization; no automatic publication.
6. Separate product notices for privacy, commerce, AI, community copyright and minors when applicable; no universal boilerplate assumption.
7. Incident reporting deadlines independent of owner alert acknowledgment, with real legal applicability.
8. Budget new recurring ₹5,000 planning ceiling, ₹2,500/₹4,000 warnings; every paid activation needs owner approval.
9. Original /corner, /track, existing terms, privacy, analytics/contact functionality preserved.
10. No high/critical blockers in affected release. Four separate engineering/release/production/legal approval states.

## Exclusions until separately verified
No new operative policy, no regulated feature activation, no payment/mailer sends, no company data, no auto legal conclusions, no production migration, no uncontracted monitoring/warranty.
