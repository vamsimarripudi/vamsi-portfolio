Celebrate beautifully.
Celebrate safely.

Six thoughtful precautions for a festival filled with light, joy and care.

01 / 06

Stay close to children.

Children should always celebrate under the supervision of a responsible adult.

02 / 06

Choose approved fireworks.

Use only legally approved fireworks, and carefully follow their safety instructions.

03 / 06

Keep water close.

Keep water or sand nearby, and stay prepared for accidental fires.

04 / 06

Mind your surroundings.

Never burst crackers near vehicles, houses or flammable objects.

05 / 06

Care for our air.

Limit smoke and loud noise. Consider quieter celebrations for everyone.

06 / 06 · OUR SPECIAL PROMISE

Protect street dogs.

Before lighting crackers, look for dogs nearby. Never scare, chase, or harm them.

✦ Every little act of care makes the festival brighter. ✦

EVERY LIFE DESERVES PEACE
A MESSAGE FROM THE HEART

Let the streets shine.
Not tremble.

Diwali is a celebration of happiness. But sudden bangs and loud fireworks can frighten street dogs who have nowhere to hide.

Before lighting anything, check beneath parked vehicles and around nearby shelters. Give animals a safe distance. Never throw a cracker toward them.

Your celebration should never become their fear.
