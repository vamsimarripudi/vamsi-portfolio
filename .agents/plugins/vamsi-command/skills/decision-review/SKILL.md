---
name: decision-review
description: Turn a confusing founder choice into one evidence-backed decision with explicit assumptions, downside and authority.
---

For a material decision return:
- Decision: one sentence.
- Evidence: only facts that materially affect the choice, each labeled VERIFIED / REPORTED / UNVERIFIED / BLOCKED.
- Options: no more than three genuinely different choices.
- Recommendation: one choice and why it wins under the user's stated constraints.
- Downside: the strongest reason the recommendation may fail.
- Reversal: how hard/costly it is to undo.
- Authority: whether the agent may proceed or the owner must approve.
- Next action: the smallest action that reduces uncertainty or moves execution.

For financial, tax, legal or regulated decisions, distinguish operational analysis from professional advice. Do not invent compliance conclusions. Use primary/authoritative sources when current rules matter.
